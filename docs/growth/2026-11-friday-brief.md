## The maths to 50 bags
50 bags a month is about 12 a week. Two kinds of buyer get him there very differently.

| Buyer | Bags per order **(assumption)** | Orders needed for 50 bags | Enquiries needed if 1 in 3 buys **(assumption)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowners only | 1–2 | 25–50 | 75–150 a month |
| Landscapers who reorder | 4–6 a month each | 8–12 regular customers | about 30 conversations, once |

What this means:

- **Free Google search alone won't reach 50 soon.** 64 visits a month cannot produce 75–150 enquiries. Search is
  the slow engine: worth building, but it pays off over months.
- **Ten regular landscapers is most of the goal.** That is a list and some phone calls, not a marketing campaign.
- **Bag count isn't the same as money.** A pebble bag sells for $800–$1,100; soil and mulch sell for $128–$178.
  One pebble bag brings in as much as 6 soil bags. Pebble pages already get most of the product clicks. Ask which
  he actually makes money on.

## The three free moves
1. **Trade customers.** I'm building a list of landscapers and turf layers within about 20 km of the yard. Ronnie
   (or I, with his okay) contacts them with a simple first-order offer. The site already takes trade applications.
2. **Urban Pots customers.** Everyone who bought a pot needs soil, and many want pebbles on top. One message to
   that list. He already owns this audience.
3. **Follow up every quote.** Delivery is quoted per job, so every enquiry becomes a quote, and quotes that get no
   follow-up die. This is the system I build for trades. ULS would be the first one running.

Search and the Google listing keep growing in the background (Northern Beaches pages, turf underlay, Wetherill Park).

## Targets to agree on
- First paid bag order within **14 days**.
- **25 bags** in a calendar month by day 60.
- **50 bags** in a calendar month by day 90.

Judged on his own order records, not on rankings. If we miss, we'll both see the real number and why.

   deciding?)

4. Does he want 50 bags, or does he want a profit number? (If margin is thin on soil, 20 pebble bags may beat 50

9. Will he ask every customer for a Google review? I'll give him a one-line text to send.

**Delivery facts for the site**

    Google impressions, so the promise has to be true.
